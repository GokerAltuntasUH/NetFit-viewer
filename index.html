How is the score calculated?

Score = −(0.4 × intersection penalty length + 0.6 × off-site penalty length). Each road is counted in both stored directions. A building contact contributes at least 0.0001, including a road node's self-edge when it touches or enters a building. The length cards above count each physical road once.

A positive reward means the last move improved the score. Connected segments follow the moved node; the road connections do not change. Boundary nodes can move off-site. Coordinates use the original 32-bit movement precision.
